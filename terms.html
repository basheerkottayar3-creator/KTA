Institutional Governance & Commercial Policy

Commercial Terms of Trade.

Institutional supply standards, wholesale packaging specifications, 2–24h replenishment SLAs, 10-day credit protocols, and custom sourcing governance for KTA procurement partners.

1. Minimum Orders (500kg+) 2. Hotel Smart (2–24h SLAs) 3. Credit Terms (10 Days) 4. Custom Sourcing & Pricing 5. Chef Welcome Box 6. 48-Hour Inspection & Returns 7. Transit & Freight POD 8. Natural Crop Variations 9. Limitation of Liability 10. Chennai Jurisdiction

1. Institutional Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) & Packaging Standards

KTA operates direct single-origin agricultural processing and supply infrastructure tailored exclusively for commercial kitchens, luxury hotel chains, institutional caterers, food processors, and industrial extraction units.

  • Standard Wholesale Minimum: 500 kg minimum consignment across single or mixed spice varieties.
  • Adaptive Packaging Tailored with Customers: In addition to standard consignment units, KTA provides fully flexible, customer-adaptive packaging tailored to institutional buyer specifications — including 1kg aroma-barrier chef pouches, 5kg foodservice tins, 25kg multi-layer sacks, 40kg heavy-duty master bags, 25–50kg fiber drums, and 500kg–1000kg FIBC jumbo bulk sacks.
  • Standard Consignment Master Bags: Heavy-duty food-grade multi-layer master bags (25kg & 40kg) moisture-barrier sealed with inner food-grade liners on shrink-wrapped pallets.
  • Chef & Kitchen Foodservice Standard: 1kg aroma-barrier pouches and 5kg foodservice tins for active culinary pass prep and menu staging.
  • Industrial & Extraction Bulk Tonnage: Supplied in multi-ton container loads, fiber drum packs, or FIBC bulk sacks upon verified specification.

2. Hotel Smart 24/7 Replenishment Protocols & SLAs

Commercial kitchens, hotel properties, and enterprise hospitality groups registered under the KTA Hotel Smart program receive priority order execution and dedicated buffer inventory reservations:

  • 2–24 Hour Guaranteed Priority Dispatch: Rapid replenishment dispatched directly to the partner property's receiving dock across active operational delivery corridors.
  • Zero-Stockout Buffer Guarantee: Dedicated reserve stocks are held in buffer warehousing for contracted hospitality accounts to insulate against seasonal supply volatility, festival demand spikes, and banquet surges.
  • 100% Unadulterated Origin Purity: All KTA consignments are Highland Specific, sourced and graded by KTA alone. Guaranteed zero synthetic anti-caking agents, zero lead chromate, zero artificial polish, and zero spent biomass blending.
Single-Origin Grading Standard: Every lot is procured directly from growers and auction floors and evaluated by KTA specialists to guarantee pure physical characteristics, natural essential oil levels, and clean culinary performance.

3. Commercial Credit Facilities & Institutional Settlement (10-Day Start)

Commercial accounts are eligible for institutional revolving credit facilities upon completing standard trade onboarding and credit verification:

  • Initial Credit Cycle (10 Days): Approved commercial buyers, hotel chains, and restaurant enterprises start on a 10-day revolving credit term from the date of physical delivery confirmation.
  • Credit Term Expansion: Following consistent quarterly trade history, compliance with payment cycles, and trade review, credit terms may be upgraded to 15 or 30 days upon mutual agreement.
  • First-Time & Trial Consignments: Initial commercial consignments and trial orders (500kg MOQ) are cleared against advance commercial pro-forma invoice or digital banking confirmation prior to warehouse gate release.
  • Settlement Channels: Corporate RTGS, NEFT, direct electronic bank transfer, or Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC) for multi-ton/export contracts.

4. Custom Sourcing, Specialty Commodities & Market-Pegged Pricing

KTA provides tailored custom sourcing services for culinary commodities, rare botanicals, and bespoke grind profiles outside our standard 51-variety roster:

  • Custom Agricultural & Botanical Sourcing: On-demand procurement of Vanilla beans, Ajwain (Omam), Hing (Asafoetida), Tamarind, fresh rhizomes (Green Ginger, Raw Turmeric), oleoresins, and specialty herbs leveraging our 25+ year grower networks.
  • Custom Granulation & Mesh Milling: Whole garbled, cracked cut, coarse kibbled, and 60–100 mesh ultra-fine cold-milled powders processed to buyer specification.
  • Spot Market Pricing Policy: All commodity pricing is pegged directly to daily origin arrivals and auction floor rates with zero intermediary trader markups. Pro-forma quotations are spot-locked for 48 hours from issuance.
  • Non-Cancellable Custom Briefs: Custom sourcing orders, contract farming allocations, and bespoke milling lots are binding and strictly non-cancellable once origin procurement or physical processing has commenced.

5. Complimentary Chef Welcome Box Policy

Executive Chefs, Culinary Directors, and F&B Purchase Managers of verified hospitality establishments are eligible to request a complimentary Chef Welcome Box:

  • Sensory Evaluation Kit: Contains origin-fresh whole and cold-milled spice discovery packs (1kg pouches / discovery samples) delivered with zero sample cost and zero shipping charges.
  • Purpose & Scope: The discovery kit is provided exclusively for kitchen pass sensory trials, aroma profiling, and culinary testing. Formal batch Certificates of Analysis (COA) and complete laboratory assays are provided exclusively on commercial bulk consignments upon formal request.

6. Quality Inspection, Strict 48-Hour Claim Window & Non-Returnable Policy

Because KTA supplies perishable, food-grade agricultural commodities adhering to strict food safety protocols, the following inspection and return governance applies universally:

CRITICAL FOOD SAFETY & RETURN POLICY:
• 48-Hour Inspection Window: Buyer receiving teams maintain a strict 48-hour inspection window from the exact date and timestamp of physical carrier delivery.
• Opened & Unsealed Goods Non-Returnable: In compliance with food safety regulations, any bag, container, drum, or liner that has been unsealed, opened, sampled, cut, tampered with, or partially used CANNOT be returned, exchanged, or refunded under any circumstances.
• Deemed Acceptance: If no formal written claim (accompanied by carrier consignment note, lot batch numbers, and unboxing video evidence) is submitted to KTA Trade Desk within 48 hours of delivery, the consignment is irreversibly deemed accepted in full and in perfect merchantable condition.

7. Carrier Transit Damage & Freight Protocol

All consignments are dispatched in moisture-sealed multi-layer packaging via verified freight lines:

  • Proof of Delivery (POD) Endorsement: In the event of visible transit damage, torn outer packaging, or moisture ingress occurring during freight transit, the receiving team must endorse the damage on the carrier's physical or digital Proof of Delivery (POD) slip prior to signing.
  • Damage Reporting SLA: Transit damage must be reported to KTA Trade Desk with photographic and unboxing video evidence within 24 hours of delivery. Claims submitted without a signed carrier POD damage endorsement are barred from carrier insurance and KTA claims.

8. Natural Agricultural Product Variation Disclaimer

All KTA spices, dry fruits, and seeds are 100% natural, single-origin agricultural commodities harvested across Highland Specific terroirs:

  • Terroir Variations: Minor natural variations in grain size, botanical coloration, moisture levels (strictly within statutory FSSAI limits), and essential oil yield are inherent to genuine single-origin agricultural harvests and do not constitute a defect or breach of contract.
  • Storage Responsibility: Once accepted at destination, buyer assumes full responsibility for storing consignments in clean, cool, dry, pest-free, and moisture-controlled warehouse environments.

9. Limitation of Liability & Exclusion of Consequential Damages

  • Cap on Financial Liability: KTA's aggregate financial liability for any claim, defect, dispatch delay, or breach arising out of commercial supply shall under no circumstances exceed the net purchase invoice value of the specific defective consignment batch.
  • Consequential Damages Excluded: KTA shall not be liable for any indirect, special, incidental, punitive, or consequential losses, including but not limited to loss of commercial profit, banquet or menu disruptions, hospitality kitchen downtime, or third-party claims.

10. Force Majeure

KTA shall not be liable for failure to dispatch or delays in delivery caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including acts of God, extreme weather events, highland monsoon landslides, road closures, crop harvest failures, labor strikes, transport embargoes, pandemic lockdowns, or carrier freight disruptions.

11. Governing Law & Exclusive Jurisdiction

All commercial transactions, pro-forma invoices, supply agreements, and terms of trade shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the Republic of India.

Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to commercial procurement with KTA Spices shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
