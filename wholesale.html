Understanding "All Spices Spent" Supply
All Spices Spent refers to post-extraction biomass residues primarily composed of spent black pepper, spent dry ginger, spent turmeric, and mixed botanical spices after oleoresin, solvent, or steam extraction. Supplied in dry, moisture-controlled bulk bags for industrial re-processing, cattle feed formulation, organic fertilizer manufacturing, and biomass applications.
