Accessibility Statement

KTA Spices is committed to ensuring our digital catalogue and trade services are accessible to all culinary partners and executive buyers.

Our Commitment

At Kottayar Trading Agency (KTA), we believe commercial trade, single-origin botanical sourcing, and wholesale spice procurement should be accessible to everyone. We continually strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

Accessibility Features on This Website

We actively incorporate the following design and technical measures:

  • Semantic HTML Structure: Clear heading hierarchies, accessible landmark regions, and descriptive button labels for assistive technologies.
  • High-Contrast Typography: Readability-tested font pairings with robust contrast ratios across all lighting conditions.
  • Descriptive Alt Text: Meaningful alternative text descriptions on all spice harvests, product packaging, and certificates.
  • Keyboard & Touch Navigation: Full keyboard navigability, focus indicators, and responsive touch targets designed for mobile and tablet passes.
  • Responsive Layouts: Clean scaling across screen sizes, zoom levels (up to 200%), and high-density retina displays without loss of content.
