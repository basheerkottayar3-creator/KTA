Questions & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions.

Simple, clear answers for Executive Chefs, Hotel Purchasing Heads, and Restaurant Owners.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale?
+
Our institutional wholesale supply operates with standard packaging starting from 40kg master bags and consignments (tailored across single or mixed spice varieties for hotels, cloud kitchens, and institutional buyers, up to multi-ton lots). Products are sealed in heavy-duty food-grade master packaging. Minimum wholesale procurement is 500kg+. For verified Executive Chefs and Hotel Purchase Directors testing new menu lots, our Spice Discovery Samples are coordinated directly with our culinary desk.
How fast is delivery across South India?
+
Under our Hotel Smart 24/7 logistics network, replenishment orders placed by partnered commercial kitchen accounts are dispatched within 2 to 24 hours with moisture-locked protection straight to your receiving bay. Discovery sample consignments are dispatched based on geographic kitchen location.
Are KTA spices batch-tested and single-origin certified?
+
Yes. Every consignment carries an origin lot number, moisture threshold analysis, and FSSAI certificate. Sourced and graded by KTA alone, we never blend harvests or add artificial polishing agents.
How do Executive Chefs receive Spice Discovery Samples?
+
Simply visit the Partnership page, enter your property name and contact number, and our culinary trade desk will coordinate sample dispatch according to your kitchen location. For enrolled Hotel Smart partners, replenishment dispatches are guaranteed within 2 to 24 hours.
Do you offer strategic sourcing, custom grinding, or pepper byproducts?
+
Yes. We handle end-to-end institutional procurement, custom coarse/fine grinding specifications, whole peppercorns, pepper husk/pinheads, and rare single-origin botanicals.
What payment and credit terms are available for institutional accounts?
+
We offer flexible billing structures, quarterly locked volume contracts to protect your food cost margins, and customized credit terms for verified commercial hospitality accounts after initial onboarding.
